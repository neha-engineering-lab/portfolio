How I approach technology

I build at the intersection of enterprise software, healthcare and AI. My foundation is more than a decade of experience designing and delivering complex software systems; my current focus is extending that foundation into AI engineering — exploring how agentic workflows, retrieval, evaluation and human oversight can solve meaningful enterprise problems.

My AI work is intentionally grounded in real healthcare and insurance use cases such as member eligibility, enrollment, claims and service operations. I approach these as product and engineering problems: first defining the opportunity and desired outcome, then architecture, evaluation, tradeoffs, risk and production readiness — with implementation following.
